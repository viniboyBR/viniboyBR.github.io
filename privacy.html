Privacy policy

MODEL is a personal app used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar: creates and reads events in calendars the owner shares with it.
  • Google Tasks: creates, reads and deletes tasks in one task list it creates.
  • Gmail, read-only: reads messages in its own mailbox to find apartment-listing alert emails. It cannot send, change or delete mail.
  • Google Drive: only files it creates itself (a spreadsheet of listings).

Where the data goes

Data read from Google is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access tokens stay on that computer.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account under Security, Third-party connections.

Contact

The support email shown on Google's consent screen for this app.
